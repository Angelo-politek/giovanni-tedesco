Behind the shot

Equipment and software.

For the deep sky he uses a full-spectrum modified Canon EOS 500D on a 150/750 Newtonian and a 2004 Celestron CG5 GT mount. For the Moon and Saturn, a ZWO ASI120 planetary camera.

During a session a laptop runs the acquisition and the autoguiding. The individual exposures are then stacked and processed.

Equipment

  • Newtonian 150/750 mm
  • Celestron CG5 GT
  • Canon EOS 500D full-spectrum
  • Optolong L-Pro filter
  • Baader MPCC Mark II corrector
  • 60 mm guide scope + ZWO ASI120

Software

Questions.

How does reserving work?

Choose a work and a size, copy the ready-made request and send it to Giovanni on Instagram. He replies with availability and the number of your copy. You pay only after confirmation.

When will the print arrive?

Each copy is printed to order, then signed and numbered. Giovanni gives you timing and shipping cost when he confirms the reservation.

What happens when an edition sells out?

That size will never be printed again. The other sizes of the same work remain available until they sell out.

Is the print framed?

No, it arrives unframed with a white margin, ready to frame. The margin also carries the signature and number, which stay visible under the mount.
