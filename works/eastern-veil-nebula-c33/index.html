In the constellation of Cygnus, C33 (NGC 6992), the Eastern Veil Nebula, is part of the Veil Nebula, a vast supernova remnant about 2,400 light years from Earth.

Its glowing filaments of gas are what remains of a massive star that exploded thousands of years ago. The shock wave from the explosion is still travelling through the surrounding interstellar medium.
